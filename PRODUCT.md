# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

UAE architects, real-estate developers, general contractors, and high-end homeowners selecting fenestration systems for residential towers, villas, and commercial fit-outs. They are procurement-minded and specification-driven: they evaluate on technical certifications, proven project portfolios, and the credibility the brand radiates before they pick up the phone. A secondary audience is end-buyers (villa owners, apartment purchasers) who discover Emaar through developer recommendations and validate their trust online.

## Product Purpose

Emaar International Industry manufactures and installs uPVC and aluminium windows, doors, and facades for the UAE market. The website's job is to convert qualified visitors into enquiries: it must project engineering authority, validate quality credentials, and make the path to a quote frictionless. Success looks like a specifier arriving on the site and immediately trusting that this is the right manufacturer to call.

## Brand Personality

Premium · Refined · Ambitious

Voice is confident and specific, never generic. Copy reads the way a senior technical sales director would speak: direct, evidence-backed, free of superlatives. In Arabic, the register is formal Gulf Arabic, not colloquial.

## Anti-references

- Generic UAE construction company sites: stock-photo heavy, blue-and-gold palette, visual noise, low production value. This is the category default; every decision should sit clearly above it.
- Heavy ornamentation and traditional Arabic decorative style: geometric Islamic patterns, ornate borders, calligraphic flourishes used as decoration. Emaar is contemporary and technically credible; historicism reads as dated, not premium.
- SaaS or tech landing page grammar: hero metrics grids, gradient text, animated feature-card rows. Wrong industry register.

## Design Principles

1. **Engineering as aesthetic.** The visual language should feel like it came from a materials specification sheet, not a mood board. Precision in spacing, weight in type, warmth in shadows.
2. **Evidence over assertion.** Every claim should be backed by something visible: a certification logo, a project photograph, a named statistic. Never a headline without a proof point nearby.
3. **Arabic and English as equals.** RTL layout is a first-class design consideration, not an afterthought. Both languages should feel like the site was designed for them natively.
4. **Restraint that carries weight.** The palette is narrow and disciplined. The red CTA earns its power because everything around it is quiet. Resist adding visual elements unless they serve a clear function.
5. **Confident hierarchy.** One dominant idea per section. Visitors should always know what they are supposed to look at and what to do next.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA minimum. Bilingual EN/AR with full RTL layout support. Reduced-motion preferences must be respected on all Framer Motion animations. Minimum 44 px touch targets on all interactive elements. The site is used on mobile by contractors visiting from site; performance and legibility on small screens in bright UAE sunlight are real requirements.
