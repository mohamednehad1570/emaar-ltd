# Product

## Company
Emaar International Industry LLC — UAE
Manufactures and installs uPVC and aluminium windows, doors, and facades.

## Users

### Primary (specifiers)
UAE architects, real-estate developers, general contractors selecting
fenestration systems for residential towers, villas, and commercial fit-outs.
Procurement-minded and specification-driven. Evaluate on technical
certifications, proven project portfolios, and brand credibility before
they pick up the phone.

### Secondary (end-buyers)
Villa owners and apartment purchasers who discover Emaar through developer
recommendations and validate trust online. Less technical, more visual.
Converted by project photography and social proof.

## Website job
Convert qualified visitors into enquiries. Project engineering authority,
validate quality credentials, make the path to a quote frictionless.
Success: a specifier arrives and immediately trusts this is the right
manufacturer to call.

## Brand personality
Premium · Refined · Ambitious

## Voice
Confident and specific — never generic. Reads like a senior technical
sales director: direct, evidence-backed, free of superlatives.
In Arabic: formal Gulf Arabic, not colloquial.

## Anti-references (never look like these)
- Generic UAE construction sites: stock-photo heavy, blue-and-gold
  palette, visual noise, low production value
- Heavy Arabic ornamentation: geometric Islamic patterns, ornate borders,
  calligraphic flourishes as decoration — dated, not premium
- SaaS landing page grammar: hero metrics grids, gradient text,
  animated feature-card rows — wrong industry register

## Design principles
1. Engineering as aesthetic — feels like a materials spec sheet,
   not a mood board. Precision in spacing, weight in type.
2. Evidence over assertion — every claim backed by something visible:
   a cert logo, a project photo, a named statistic.
3. Arabic and English as equals — RTL is first-class, not afterthought.
4. Restraint that carries weight — narrow palette, red earns its power
   because everything around it is quiet.
5. Confident hierarchy — one dominant idea per section.

## Accessibility
WCAG 2.1 AA minimum. Full RTL. Reduced-motion respected.
44px minimum touch targets. Mobile performance critical —
contractors visit from construction sites on mobile in UAE sunlight.
